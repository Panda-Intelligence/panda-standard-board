# E10 — v0.2.6: lower-cost, lower-profile parts on the 4.7-inch board (2026-11-17)

User direction (2026-11-17): push cost, part count and thickness down while keeping quality and performance. Of the
five proposals the user approved A, B and C. v0.2.6 is v0.2.5 with those three; the circuit is unchanged.

| | Change | Parts | Cost per board | Height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 22 µF 0805 / 25 V → 22 µF 0603 / 10 V (CCTC TCC0603X5R226M100CT, C20416425), the part already at C401–C403 and C501 | C207, C208 (VSYS at the charger), C525 (3V3_RF) | 3 × 0.148 → 3 × 0.011 | about 1.45 → 0.9 mm |
| B | 10 µF 1206 / 50 V → 10 µF 0805 / 25 V (FH 0805X106K250NT, C40894), the part already at C206 | C203 (charger PMID) | 0.197 → 0.072 | about 1.8 → 1.45 mm |
| C | M1.6 nut 2.0 mm → 1.0 mm high (Sinhoo SMTSO1610MTJ, C2928167; 3851 in JLC stock) | H2, H3 | 2 × 0.051 → 2 × 0.073 | 2.0 → 1.0 mm |

- **Cost**: −$0.49 per board (JLC prices near 1000 pieces, read 2026-11-17). BOM lines 75 → 73.
- **Height**: the nuts were the tallest parts together with the microSD socket. After v0.2.6 the socket J501 (about
  2.0 mm) alone sets the ceiling; next are C923 (1206, about 1.8 mm), the keys (about 1.5 mm) and J302 (1.4 mm).
  Heights are catalogue figures, not measured. The body gets thinner only when the socket changes too (proposal E).
- **Board**: four footprints exchanged in place, same position and rotation (C207 / C208 moved 0.025 mm so that the
  ground pad edge stays where it was). No track or via changed; every track end that sat on an old pad sits on the
  new pad (checked by `v026/eco_cost.py`). Same nut footprint.
- **Checks** (KiCad 10.0.7, `v0.2.6/`): ERC 0, DRC 0, 0 unconnected, 0 parity. Order pack: 160 placements, 73 BOM
  lines, 444 vias, 22 via-in-pad.

To check on the first boards:

  charging and at a load step, and the RF supply. Not calculated.
- C: thread length in the nut is halved. Ask the toolmaker and pull-test the fixing; E07 §7 open points still apply.
