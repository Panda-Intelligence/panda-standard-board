# E11 — v0.2.7: 1.45 mm microSD socket and 0805 front-light capacitor (2026-11-17)

User decision (2026-11-17): keep a push-push socket, take a lower one. v0.2.7 is v0.2.6 with two part changes; the
circuit is unchanged.

| Ref | Was | Now | Height | Cost (JLC, ~1000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J501 | XUNPU TF-122-CCP9, push-push | Hanbo TF-019 (C961680, 1302 in stock), push-push | about 2.0 → 1.45 mm (drawing) | 0.17 → 0.29 |

Part ceiling on the board after v0.2.7: about 1.5 mm (keys SW201 / SW202; then J501, C923, C203 at 1.45 mm and J302
at 1.4 mm). It was 2.0 mm. Catalogue and drawing figures, not measured. Cost: +$0.13 per board.

## 1. Socket

- **Land pattern** from the Hanbo drawing (`v027/eco_sd.py`). TF-019 has its eight terminals at the card mouth, not
  at the rear, and the body front is 1.25 mm in front of the terminal tips.
- **Pin order** checked three ways: with the card label up and the contact end away from the viewer, pin 1 is on the
  right (TF-122 and TF-018 drawings print the numbers; all three drawings put the detect switch beside pin 8). On the
  board DAT2 (pin 1) stays at the larger y, as with TF-122. The pin-field offset from the card centre also matches
  (0.63 mm on TF-122, 0.72 mm on TF-019, both towards pin 8). The drawing itself does not print terminal numbers on
  the pattern, so check the first article with a card before a volume order.
- **Card detect**: one pad; the switch closes to the shell (ground) with a card in. SD_CD and its pull-up are unchanged.
- **Position**: card centre line unchanged (board y 99.25). Body front at x −0.36, 0.86 mm over the board edge;
  terminals 0.4 mm inside the edge. No neighbouring part moved.
- **Keep-outs** from the drawing are rule areas on the front layer: the pattern-prohibited area under the slider and
  the switch keep-out. One P47_VCOM_EN via and one old ground via were inside and are gone / re-laid.
- **Routing**: SD_D0–D3, SD_CMD, SD_CLK, SD_CD, 3V3_SD and a short piece of P47_VCOM_EN were laid again by the grid
  router (`v027/spec_sd.json`); the terminals are now on the far side from the MCU, so SD_CLK is 58.7 mm with 4 vias
  (was 49.1 mm, 3), SD_CMD 62.2 mm, SD_D0 57.0 mm. No other net changed (compared item by item).

**Card protrusion is reduced, not solved.** The latched card ends 2.4 mm in front of the body. With the body front at
x −0.36 the card end is at x −2.76, about 1.3 mm outside the 67 mm body outline (2.1 mm before). A flush or recessed
card needs the socket about 2 mm further in (terminals 16.8 mm deep), which collides with R524, C806, R809, R808,
TH801, C513 and Q901: a re-layout of the left side of the panel-power block. Not done; an earlier estimate of "about
2 mm, a few small parts" in the discussion was too low.

## 2. Tool fixes found on the way

- `v027/prune_stubs.py`: a via that only joined two tracks on one layer was removed as "joining fewer than two
  layers", which cut the live SD_CD path. Such a via is now replaced by a direct link first. A last pass cuts
  overshooting track ends back to their T-junction. The v025 / v030 copies keep the old rule; their boards were
  checked (0 unconnected) and are unaffected.
- `v023/widen_v023.py`: `PANDA47_WIDEN_ONLY` limits widening to the named nets (here 3V3_SD), and keep-out
  violations now step a track down like clearance violations.
