# E08 — v0.3.0: the 4.26-inch SPI-panel board (2026-11-17)

User decision (2026-11-17): two products with different screens on one board outline.

| | Panda Standard 4.26 | Panda Standard 4.7 |
|---|---|---|
| Board | **v0.3.0 / E08** (this note), `v0.3.0/Panda426-v030.kicad_pcb` | v0.2.4 / E07, unchanged |
| Panel | 4.26" 800 × 480, 219 PPI, SPI, driver on the glass (SSD1677) | 4.7" 1216 × 684, 16-bit parallel |
| Panel supply | one boost stage on the board, rails made by the panel driver | SY7636A |
| Panel connector | J830, 24-pin 0.5 mm | J820, 40-pin 0.5 mm |
| Parts to buy | 137 placements, 67 BOM lines | 158 placements, 75 BOM lines |

Both counts come from the netlists with the same rule (parts with an MPN, test pads excluded).

Status: `manufacturing_release = false`. Native checks pass (§5). Nothing was ordered, powered or measured.

## 1. What is reused

v0.3.0 is the v0.2.4 board file with one block exchanged; it is not a new layout.

- Same outline (63 × 48 mm, speaker notch, USB-C notch, R4 corners), stack-up, rules and net classes.
- Unchanged, with their hand-laid copper: charger, 3V3 buck, front-light boost, RF, crystal, audio, microSD, keys,
  RTC, accelerometer, fuel gauge, expander, USB-C, the fixing nuts, locating holes and fiducials.
- Unchanged connectors: J803 (touch), J804 (front light), J302 (pack), J507 (antenna), J501, J201.
- Same MCU (bare ESP32-S3R8), so one firmware tree serves both boards; only the display driver and pin map differ.

## 2. What changed

Removed (45 parts): U810 and its 37 passives, diodes and inductors; J820; R726; the three SY7636A input capacitors;
test pads TP804–TP806.

Added (18 parts), values from the panel datasheet §8.2:

New BOM lines: J830, L831, Q831, R831, R832. BS1 (pin 8) is tied to ground: 4-wire SPI.

Moved or changed to make room for J830:

- C524 (3V3_RF 100 nF): 0.4 mm up.
- R510 (CHIP_PU pull-up): next to L507. C510 (CHIP_PU 1 µF): now 0402, under R510.
- C209 (VBUS 1 µF at the TVS): now 0402, 0.9 mm lower, so the panel tail clears it.
- TP6 (3V3_SD): 0.16 mm left, clear of the connector hold-down pad.
- TP801 / TP802 now carry PREVGH / PREVGL, TP803 carries BUSY.

## 4. Layout

- **J830 position** (32.0, 100.05), mouth towards the bottom edge. The panel drawing shows the tail centred on the
  glass (12.5 mm wide, 24 mm long, contacts on the display side), so the connector sits on the USB-C axis and the tail
  folds straight back over the USB-C area onto the board front. About 17 mm of the tail is used.
- **Boost stage** in the area the SY7636A left (x 16–31, y 86–96), hand-laid: L831 above Q831, the drain 1 mm from
  the inductor pad, the sense resistor beside the source, GDR / RESE 4 mm straight to pins 2 / 3. The stage takes
  3.3 V from the buck trunk at (30.7, 85.1), not from the RF supply branch.
- **VSH2** capacitor C836 sits on pins 5 and 8.
- **Other rail capacitors** (VSH1, VSL, VCOM, VDD) and the PREVGH / PREVGL returns are 17–19 mm of track from pins
  18–24. Those pins face the RF supply and the MCU, where no 0603 fits. Track widths there are 0.127–0.4 mm.
- Grid router (`v030/spec_display.json`, converged in 5–7 iterations): six panel signals, six rails, three strap
  nets, CHIP_PU, FL_FB and the status-LED feed (VSYS_RAW to R208, which used to run through SY7636A pads).

Tool note: `v030/prune_stubs.py` removes feed branches of deleted parts. Its first version cut a live I2C trunk
because one track end overshot a T-junction by 0.18 mm. It now removes a track only when one end touches nothing and
no other track ends on its body. `eco_strip.py` also links track ends that met only on a removed pad.

## 5. Checks

KiCad 10.0.7, in `v0.3.0/`: ERC 0. DRC 0 violations, 0 unconnected, 0 schematic parity (`--severity-all`).
The generated schematic's netlist equals the source netlist (143 nets). The v0.2.4 netlist and project are unchanged.

`v030/build_v030.sh` rebuilds the project from v0.2.4 in one run and ends with the same result. The router is
deterministic for a given input, unlike the Freerouting step of v0.2.4.

## 6. Open

1. **Panel module.** Pinout, circuit and tail position come from the bare-panel datasheet (GDEQ0426T82, which the
   maker lists as end of life). The module with front light and touch (105.33 × 62.37 × 1.98 mm) must be confirmed:
   current part number, tail position and length, touch controller and its tail, front-light tail, LED string
   voltage and current against the SGM3732 circuit. J803 / J804 keep the unverified v0.2.4 poses.
2. **Tail path.** The tail lies over C209, D201, D202, R206, R207, the MH1 pin tip and the USB-C shell (about 1.2 mm
   above the board). No 3D check was done. J830's land pattern is scaled from the 40-pin part of the same series,
   not taken from the 24-pin drawing.
3. **Rail capacitors 17–19 mm from the pins** (§4). Check ripple on VSH1 / VSL / VCOM and image quality on the first
   boards; the fallback is 0402 capacitors on the back under J830.
4. **Capacitance under bias.** The datasheet asks for 4.7 µF / 25 V; the 0603 parts keep a fraction of that at
   ±15–20 V. Not calculated.
5. **Q831.** Chosen from catalogue data as the equivalent of the datasheet's MOSFET; pin order (1 G, 2 S, 3 D) is
   the usual SOT-323 one and was not read from the Winsok datasheet.
6. **RF.** C524 moved 0.4 mm and the 3V3_AON feed to L507 was re-laid around R831. Small, but RF was not re-checked.
7. **Enclosure.** Not started. Estimates only: body about 67.4 × 125.6 × 6.5 mm, cell bay about 8 mm shorter than
   the 4.7-inch one (pack about 2.5 × 57 × 71 mm).
8. All open items of E07 §6 still apply to the shared circuit.
