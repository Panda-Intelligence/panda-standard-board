# E05 v0.2.2 — rounded corners, port layout, lower parts, status LED (2026-11-12)

v0.2.2 is v0.2.1 (E04) with a new port layout, rounded board corners, a lower flash and a status LED. Apart from the
two new parts (§4), the circuit, rules and footprints are those of v0.2.1. v0.2.1 stays in `v0.2.1/` as the reference.

## 1. Decisions (user, 2026-11-12)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Device size | Keep the size class, with larger corners: R6, so the device grows to 118.3 × 67.0 × 7.3 mm (was 117.5 × 66.2). Above about 4.6 mm, an unchanged outline leaves too thin a wall between the R6 corner and the 62 × 113.45 mm glass. |
| Board size | "At least 70 × 70 mm" does not fit the device: the inside is 64 mm wide, and the 70 mm cell leaves about 44 mm of length. So the board is made as large as fits, 63 × 43 mm, and the spare area carries printed information. |
| Board corners | The two bottom corners get an R4 radius. |
| Thickness | Keep the 3.2 mm cell, so the device stays about 7.3 mm. The cell sets the thickness; part heights only set the margin. Parts are kept as low as practical. |
| Ports | SD slot on the left edge, both keys on the right edge, USB-C centred on the bottom edge. |
| Status LED | One LED on the charger STAT pin (§4). |
| Reset | No pinhole. SW202 is already wired to SGM41513 nQON: holding it for 10 s on battery (8–15 s per the datasheet) cuts system power for 320 ms and restarts everything. |

## 2. Outline and placement (device-cavity coordinates, mm, y down)

- **Board**: x 0.5–63.5, y 72.4–115.4. Bottom corners are R4 arcs centred at (4.5, 111.4) and (59.5, 111.4). The
  USB-C notch is x 27.38–36.62 from y 109.85 down to the edge. The outline arcs are written as Edge.Cuts arcs

Placement trials b1–b8 kept J820 bottom left or the MCU at +14 mm. Freerouting then left 4–15 connections open:
the 16-bit panel bus crossed the PMIC block, and the SD, PGOOD and I²C lines all crossed it too.

## 3. Routing

1. **Freerouting**, 50 passes on `layout/placement_v022.json` (candidate b9): 2 connections left open.
   - USB_DM: the A7/B7 pads interleave with D+.
   - FL_C_EN: no escape from U601 pin 16.
2. **`usb_fix_v022.py`**, the hand-laid USB-C crossover:
   - D+ A6 drops through a via at (31.75, 108.0) to the existing In2 D+ track.
   - D− A7 runs up between the two D+ vias (0.16 mm clearance on each side) and joins B7 at y 107.3.
   - CC1 is re-laid on F.Cu right of A7, and on B.Cu at y 107.4 above the new via.
   - R206 moves 0.35 mm up.
3. **Grid router** (`layout/v022_local_spec.json`): it re-lays all of FL_C_EN. It also re-lays SD_CD, IMU_INT1,
   KEY_POWER, FUEL_GPOUT, EN_3V3_SD, BQ_INT and P47_HV_EN, but only inside a 3.9 × 6.6 mm window at U601's right edge.
   It converged at iteration 5 with no conflicts. No power, RF or crystal net was ripped.
4. **Clean-up**: `finish_v020.py`, prune and trim of dangling ends, `finish_v020.py` again (one dangling via
   removed). Then `silk_v020.py` and `info_silk_v02x.py`.
5. **Order-pack review**: a GND stitch via inside C505 pad 2 (0402) was removed. The pad stays tied to GND by its
   F.Cu tracks and the pour, which brings the via-in-pad count back to 17, as in v0.2.1.

| U901 pin 4 (STAT) | not connected | CHG_STAT | Open drain: low while charging, high when done, 1 Hz blink on a fault. Firmware can force on / blink via STAT_SET when EN_ICHG_MON = 01 (SGM41513 datasheet). |

Parts that were checked and **not** changed:
- **TS-1186E-B-B keys**: XKB drawing A1 gives a body height of 1.50 ± 0.10 mm. The 3.55 mm "H" is the depth
  including the actuator, so the keys do not reach the screen stack. No lower side-push switch was found.
- **TF-122-CCP9 microSD**: LCSC lists 1.8 mm. The JMEconn 5001B-TF128-054 (C53278151, 1.28 mm, 85 in stock) was found,
  but its footprint could not be cross-checked against vendor CAD, so it is kept as a candidate.
- **The two 1206 capacitors (C203, C923)**: about 1.6 mm, already below the keys and the SD socket. Swapping them gains no
  height and costs derating.

After the flash change, the tallest board parts are the SD socket (1.8–2.0 mm) and the keys (1.5 mm). All parts
stay under the 2.7 mm gap between the board top (z 1.6) and the steel plate (z 4.3).
